Cleaning – Clinical Contact Surfaces

These surfaces are touched frequently during patient care and can become contaminated. They include:

  • Dental chair headrest and controls
  • Overhead light handles
  • Instrument trays and handpiece holders
  • Computer keyboard and mouse
  • Triplex syringe handles
  • Suction controls
Disinfection – Housekeeping Surfaces

These are less likely to be touched during treatment but still need routine cleaning. Examples include:

  • Floors
  • Walls
  • Sinks
